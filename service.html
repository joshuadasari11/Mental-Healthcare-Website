Digital Wellness Resources

Empower yourself with curated information, scientific studies, and digital tracking tools designed to help reduce screen dependance, restore concentration, and promote healthier daily habits.

Tools & Tech
Screen Limit Apps

Leverage technology to limit technology. These top-rated apps can actively block distractions and gamify your focus:

  • Opal (iOS): Blurs distracting apps, tracks screen habits, and lets you set strict schedule locks.
  • Forest (iOS/Android): Gamifies focus. Plant a digital seed that grows into a tree only if you stay off your phone.
  • Freedom (Cross-Platform): Blocks specific websites and apps across your phone, tablet, and computer simultaneously.
  • Google Family Link: Excellent for parents to set daily screen limits and monitor kid's app installations.
Literature
Books & Studies

Expand your knowledge on the attention economy and smartphone psychology with these essential reads:

  • "Digital Minimalism" by Cal Newport: A philosophy to focus online activities on valued behaviors.
  • "How to Break Up with Your Phone" by Catherine Price: A practical 30-day plan to rewire screen habits.
  • "Frontiers in Psychiatry (2021)": Study showing smartphone overuse linked directly to anxiety and attention drops in teens.
Health Habits
Physical Wellbeing

Guard your body and sleep hygiene against screen strain. Apply these digital health adjustments today:

  • The 20-20-20 Rule: Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to prevent digital eye strain.
  • Blue-Light Management: Block blue light 1-2 hours before sleep to ensure natural melatonin secretion.
  • Postural Hygiene: Avoid "Text Neck" by holding your screen at eye level rather than tilting down.
