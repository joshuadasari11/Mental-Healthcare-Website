Who Are We

At Addiction on phone , we are passionate about addressing the growing concern of phone addiction among children. Our mission is to create awareness, provide resources, and empower parents, educators, and communities to foster healthier relationships with technology

We Are KLE TECH STUDENTS

We believe that while technology is a powerful tool, its overuse can have significant consequences on a child's mental health, social development, and academic performance. Our goal is to strike a balance between the benefits of technology and the importance of real-world interactions. Through research-based insights, practical tips, and engaging content, we aim to: Educate families about the risks and signs of phone addiction. Offer actionable strategies to reduce screen time. Promote activities that encourage meaningful offline engagement.
