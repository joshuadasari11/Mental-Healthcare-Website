About Our Mission

At Digital Well-being Initiative (DTSI), we are passionate about addressing the growing concern of screen and smartphone addiction among children and teenagers. Our goal is to create awareness, offer practical digital-wellness resources, and empower families, educators, and communities to strike a healthy balance between technology and real-world interactions.

Our Core Purpose

Empowering Digital Balance

While technology acts as a powerful learning tool, its excessive, unmonitored use can lead to adverse effects on cognitive development, sleep hygiene, and mental health. As students of **KLE Technological University**, we developed this project to actively address this modern challenge.

  • Raise Awareness: Education on signs and dangers of dependency.
  • Actionable Solutions: Giving parents and teachers real-world strategies.
  • Digital Detox: Advancing the benefits of screen-free routines.
  • Mental Well-being: Reclaiming presence, focus, and face-to-face connection.

Nomophobia Signs

Recognizing Nomophobia

Nomophobia (*No Mobile Phone Phobia*) represents the psychological dependence on digital connections. Watch out for these symptoms:

  • Phantom Vibrations: Checking the phone constantly, believing it buzzed.
  • Intense Anxiety: Panic or stress when signal is lost or battery drops below 20%.
  • Social Avoidance: Preferring screen messaging over in-person conversations.
  • Sleep Disruptions: Keeping the device under or next to the pillow, waking to check alerts.
  • Neglecting Obligations: Homework, hobbies, or active play are bypassed for scrolling.
